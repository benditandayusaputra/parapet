Prepared payments wait for your one-tap approval. The Policy tab changes the tiers and caps for anything processed afterwards. The Audit log tab shows the hash-chained record.

Permission policy

Owner-set authority. Tiers only ever lower what the agent may do. A failed handshake is always held; risk at or above the threshold is always escalated.
0 Observe alert only · 1 Recommend propose with reasons · 2 Prepare draft, owner approves with one tap · 3 Execute act within caps and below the risk threshold.
Changes apply to invoices processed after saving. Reset the queue to replay with a new policy.

Every step, hash-chained
